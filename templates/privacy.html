Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Taskmate ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our web platform.

2. Information We Collect

  • Personal Identification Data: Name, email address, phone number, and account password provided during registration.
  • Location Data: Real-time or inputted location details to facilitate accurate matching with local service providers.
  • Transaction Data: Payment details processed securely through our gateway partner, Razorpay. Note that we do not store sensitive credit/debit card credentials on our servers.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To create and manage your user or worker account.
  • To match service requests with qualified local providers.
  • To send critical operational notifications, such as OTP verification codes and task updates via SMS or email.
  • To process secure payments and handle payouts.

4. Data Security

We implement robust administrative, technical, and physical security measures to protect your personal information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out to us at support@taskmate.com.
